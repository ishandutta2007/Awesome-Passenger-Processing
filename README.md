# Awesome-Passenger-Processing

## Top Passenger Processing Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Airport Biometrics, Self-Service Check-in, Seamless Journey, Border Control & Identity Management*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS/enterprise platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Passenger Processing**. These systems support biometric identification, self-service kiosks, bag drop, security and border gates, boarding, and end-to-end seamless passenger journeys at airports and borders.



**Examples** include SITA Smart Path, Amadeus Self-Service / Seamless solutions, Collins Aerospace (ARINC), Vision-Box, Materna IPS, Idemia Travel, Daon IdentityX, Airware, ICTS Europe, and AeroCloud (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Production-grade passenger processing and biometric border systems are almost entirely proprietary due to aviation security, regulatory, and certification requirements. Useful open building blocks exist in biometrics, face recognition, and identity verification. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the large gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[SITA Smart Path](https://www.sita.aero/)**  

  Leading biometric passenger processing platform enabling seamless, token-based journeys from check-in through boarding and border control.



- **[Amadeus Self-Service / Seamless solutions](https://amadeus.com/)**  

  Comprehensive passenger service and biometric gate solutions supporting self-service check-in, bag drop, and contactless identification flows.



- **[Collins Aerospace (ARINC)](https://www.collinsaerospace.com/)**  

  Airport and airline passenger processing systems, including common-use platforms and related operational technology.



- **[Vision-Box](https://www.vision-box.com/)**  

  Biometric identity and seamless travel solutions widely deployed for border control, e-gates, and passenger flow management.



- **[Materna IPS](https://www.materna-ips.com/)**  

  Passenger processing and self-service solutions for check-in, bag drop, and airport operations.



- **[Idemia Travel](https://www.idemia.com/)**  

  Biometric and identity solutions for airports and borders, including facial recognition and automated border control.



- **[Daon IdentityX](https://www.daon.com/)**  

  Multi-modal biometric identity platform used in travel and high-security identity verification scenarios.



- **[Airware](https://www.airware.com/)** (or relevant regional providers)  

  Passenger and airport technology solutions supporting processing and operational workflows.



- **[ICTS Europe](https://www.icts.eu/)**  

  Security and passenger processing services and technology for aviation environments.



- **[AeroCloud](https://www.aerocloud.com/)**  

  Airport operations and passenger experience platform with digital and processing-related capabilities.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Open biometric platforms (e.g. OpenBiometrics and similar)](https://openbiometrics.dev/)**  

  Open-source biometric engines offering face recognition, liveness detection, and document processing that can be self-hosted for identity workflows.



- **[face_recognition and OpenCV-based systems](https://github.com/ageitgey/face_recognition)**  

  Widely used open libraries for facial recognition that power many experimental check-in and identification demos.



- **[Airport biometric and self-check-in demo projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Academic and community demos simulating facial recognition check-in, bag drop, and basic passenger flows.



- **[E-passport / RFID verification prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Open experiments combining RFID, biometric matching, and verification logic inspired by border control use cases.



- **[Self-check-in and kiosk open prototypes](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight systems demonstrating PNR-based or biometric self-service check-in interfaces.



- **[Identity and document verification open frameworks](https://github.com/)**  

  Tools for document scanning, MRZ reading, and basic identity matching that can support travel-related prototypes.



- **[Liveness detection and anti-spoofing open models](https://github.com/)**  

  Research and open models that help prevent presentation attacks in biometric systems.



- **[Passenger flow and queue open analytics](https://github.com/)**  

  Computer-vision and sensor projects focused on monitoring queues and passenger movement (non-security critical).



- **[Common-use and kiosk UI open experiments](https://github.com/)**  

  Front-end prototypes for self-service passenger interfaces.



- **[Standards and interoperability open resources](https://github.com/)**  

  Community work around ICAO-related data formats, biometric templates, and travel identity concepts (non-certified).



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using open face recognition and liveness libraries for research, training, or non-critical internal prototypes.

- Building educational or proof-of-concept self-check-in flows on top of open biometric APIs.

- Combining open document/MRZ tools with identity stores for simple verification demos.

- Accepting that certified biometric gates, departure control system (DCS) integration, bag-drop hardware, border-control compliance, and airport-wide orchestration remain exclusively commercial and heavily regulated.

- Treating open tools as components for innovation labs rather than production passenger processing.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Experiment with open biometric libraries → prototype face match + liveness → simulate check-in or gate flows in a controlled environment → integrate mock PNR/flight data → evaluate accuracy and UX. This is suitable for research, education, and internal R&D only. Production passenger processing (SITA Smart Path, Amadeus, Vision-Box, Idemia, Materna, Collins, etc.) requires certified systems, regulatory approvals, hardware integration, and airline/airport operational support.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Passenger processing and biometric border systems operate under strict aviation security, data protection, and government regulations. Open-source components are not certified for operational use in airports or borders. Deploying uncertified systems for real passenger processing can create serious security, legal, and safety risks. Always work with approved vendors and authorities. This list is not security, regulatory, or operational advice.
